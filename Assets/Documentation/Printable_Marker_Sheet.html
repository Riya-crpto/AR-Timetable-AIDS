Faculty Demonstration & Scanning Instructions:

  1. Print or Display: Print this sheet on standard A4 paper at 100% scale (or display this marker on a laptop/tablet screen without glare).
  2. Mounting: Place on a flat vertical wall (classroom door/notice board) or horizontal table.
  3. Scanning: Launch the AR app and point the phone camera at the marker from a distance of 30cm to 1 meter under good room lighting.
  4. AR Hologram Lock: Once detected, the 3D futuristic Timetable Board instantly spawns anchored directly over this marker. Move around the marker to showcase 3D depth, parallax, and rotation tracking.
